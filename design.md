# Design - Beleza em Fluxo

Sistema visual para os modelos comerciais do Beleza em Fluxo. A consistencia
fica no fluxo de demonstracao, na conversao e na acessibilidade; a variedade
fica na direcao de arte de cada segmento.

## Genre

Editorial contemporaneo orientado a conversao.

## Macrostructure family

- Essencia: retrato intimista e fluxo organico.
- Aura: protocolo clinico e composicao precisa.
- Movimento: revista dinamica e ritmo horizontal.
- Traco: portfolio autoral e narrativa de autoridade.
- Forma: luxo cinematografico e escala editorial.
- Urbano: manifesto direto e tabela de servicos dominante.

## Shared system

- Demonstration chrome is quiet and compact.
- Primary actions use high-contrast solid fills.
- Images carry the first viewport and section rhythm.
- Motion is limited to entrance, image depth and purposeful hover feedback.
- Every route supports reduced motion and narrow mobile viewports.

## Typography

- Shared body: Inter.
- Display variants: Cormorant Garamond, Inter, Space Grotesk, Playfair Display,
  Cormorant and Barlow Condensed.

## CTA voice

- Primary: decisive, short, solid.
- Secondary: text or hairline outline.

## What pages MUST share

- Demonstration bar behavior and conversion path.
- Focus treatment, motion timings and responsive safeguards.
- Clear brand signal in the first viewport.

## What pages MAY differ on

- Palette, display typography, section rhythm, image crop and macrostructure.
